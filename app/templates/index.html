      Calibrate the instrument

      Set how much each signal counts toward the final score. The four weights must add up to 100% — that total is what calculate_final_scores() uses to combine them.

      40%

      TF-IDF cosine similarity between the full resume and job description text.

      30%

      Jaccard overlap between the candidate's detected skills and the role's.

      20%

      Years of experience mentioned, normalized against a 20-year benchmark.

      10%
