Writing a job description that scores well

  • Name the tools you require, such as Python, Flask or AWS, instead of describing them loosely.
  • State the experience level in years, for example "3+ years".
  • Mention the degree you expect, if any.
  • List soft skills like communication or teamwork separately from technical ones.

      Getting reliable results

      • Use text-based PDFs. Scanned images can't be read.
      • One resume per file, named after the candidate.
      • Write experience as years, such as "4 years", so it can be picked up.
      • Add or remove candidates at any time. Results are recalculated when you run the analysis again.

      Scoring weights

      Decide how much each signal counts toward the final score. The four weights must add up to 100%.

      40%

      How closely the wording of the resume matches the job description (TF-IDF cosine similarity).

      30%

      Overlap between the candidate's detected skills and the role's skills (Jaccard index).

      20%

      Years of experience mentioned, measured against a 20-year benchmark.

      10%

      Highest degree found: PhD, then Masters, Bachelors, Diploma.

      100%

      balanced

      Choosing weights

      • Specialist role: raise skill match.
      • Senior role: raise experience.
      • Research or academic role: raise education.
      • Unsure: keep the defaults (40 / 30 / 20 / 10) and compare.

      You can come back and change the weights without re-adding anyone.

      How scoring works

      Every candidate receives four sub-scores between 0 and 100. They are combined using the weights you set, so you can always see why someone ranked where they did.

      Text similarity

      Resume and job description are cleaned, tokenized and lemmatized, then compared with TF-IDF cosine similarity.

      Skill match

      Technical and soft skills are detected in both documents. The score is the overlap between the two sets.

      Experience

      Years of experience stated on the resume, scaled against a 20-year benchmark.

      Education

      The highest qualification found, ordered PhD, Masters, Bachelors, Diploma.

      Frequently asked questions

      Does a high score mean someone should be hired?

      No. The score ranks resumes by how well they match the posting on paper. Use it to decide who to look at first, then review each candidate yourself.

      Why did a good candidate score low?

      Check whether their resume names the skills in the job description, states years of experience in numbers, and lists a degree. Open their row for the breakdown.

      Which file types can I upload?

      TXT, PDF and DOCX for both the job description and the resumes. PDFs must contain real text, not scanned images.

      Where is my data processed?

      Files are handled by the Flask server this app runs on. Use Start over in the header to clear the job description and all candidates.

      Can I save the results?

      Yes. Use Export CSV on the results page to download the full ranked table.
